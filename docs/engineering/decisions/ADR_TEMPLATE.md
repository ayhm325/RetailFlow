## 1. Context

## 2. Problem statement
What precise question must be decided? What is explicitly out of scope?

## 3. Decision drivers and constraints
List correctness, security, tenancy, reliability, performance, cost, operability, team capability, compatibility, and regulatory constraints. Identify approved vs assumed constraints.

## 4. Evidence and research
| Source | Authority/type | Version/date | Relevant claim | Applicability/limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Official documentation URL | Normative/vendor | ... | ... | ... |

Separate verified facts, assumptions, and owner preferences.

## 5. Options considered
For each option: design, benefits, drawbacks, security implications, operational burden, performance/cost implications, migration and rollback.

## 6. Decision
State the recommendation clearly. Keep status Proposed until owner approval.

## 7. Consequences
Positive, negative, neutral, and follow-on work.

## 8. Security and tenant isolation
Trust boundaries, attacker-controlled inputs, authorization, secrets, data protection, auditability, abuse cases.

## 9. Implementation plan
Components, data/API changes, compatibility, rollout, feature flags, migration, observability.

## 10. Rollback / recovery
Rollback or forward-fix plan, backup/restore dependencies, data compatibility.

## 11. Verification and acceptance
Concrete test IDs, load/security/operational checks, expected observable outcomes. No generic placeholders.
